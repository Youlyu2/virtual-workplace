# 屏幕衔接前景

`scene-edge-ivy.png` 是内置 imagegen 生成的透明像素前景，提供细木质边沿、上方藤叶和底部植物。由共享世界坐标绘制在房间与室外屏幕交界处，保留原全景取景；阴影和夜间颜色由原生渲染器处理。未使用 CLI/API。

## 完整生成提示词

Use case: background-extraction / production game foreground sprite. Reference1 is the current two-screen pixel-art panorama/cabin composition; reference2 is the exact cabin art for wood and plant style. Create ONE SEPARATE TRANSPARENT PORTRAIT FOREGROUND OVERLAY for the vertical boundary where the outdoor valley meets the cabin's left edge. It must make that boundary feel layered and organic. Output a tall 1:3 portrait PNG, TRUE alpha transparency, not a fullscene.
Composition: mostly TRANSPARENT empty space. A VERY SLIM, irregular warm honey-brown wooden cabin/window reveal sits around80–88 percent of the canvas width, with several short exposed textured wood sections rather than a solid full-height pole. A delicate trailing ivy vine drapes from the UPPER THIRD and reaches LEFT into transparent space, with a few asymmetric hanging branches and small rich green/yellow-green pixel leaf clusters. At the BOTTOM QUARTER, a handful of matching leafy fern/houseplant fronds reach left and slightly upward, with a few small vine tips. The MIDDLE40 percent should remain mostly transparent, with only two or three tiny ivy tips near the narrow wood edge. Keep a clear10percent transparent rightmargin so this can blend into the existingroom, and generous transparentleftmargin; do notfillthewholewidth. Nothing should look like a straight full-height colored stripe. No new pot, no ground, no landscape, no room wall plane, no curtains, no wide tree trunk, no picture frame. Small soft contact shading ONLY within the leaves/wood, no gray rectangle or exterior background.
Style: EXACTLY match references' ornate crisp16-bit farmingRPGpixelart, visible square pixel clusters, stepped darkbrown outlines, warm amber woodgrain and saturated forest/sage green leaves. Deliberately coarse pixels about6–10 outputpixelspercluster so it scales cleanly to~240×941 in the finalscene; not fine smooth vectorart, not3D, notphotoreal. Vines and ferns vary naturally in size and spacing, subtle highlights, not repeated symmetrical icons. This should look like a few foreground plants and a little visible wall thickness at a cabin corner, not a decorative sticker frame. It will be overlaid across the seam, mostly onto the outdoor half; keep it LIGHT and SPARSE so the continuous mountains remain visible through the middle.
Genuine transparent alpha everywhere outside these isolated silhouettes. No checkerboard baked in, no opaque background, no scene, no letters, no UI, no watermark, no comparison panels. One portrait sprite, no atlas.
