## 最终提示词

+Use case: ui-mockup / pixel game art design review. Create ONE beautiful cohesive concept design board for a macOS 'virtual workplace' scene, in the design language of Stardew Valley: authentic cozy 16-bit farming RPG pixel art, original assets, warm wood farmhouse interior, carefully shaded tile art and tiny game sprites. This must be TRUE 2D PIXEL ART with visible square pixel clusters, consistent low-resolution grid and nearest-neighbor edges. Absolutely NO 3D, NO clay toy, NO large-headed rendered chibi, NO smooth vector illustration, NO painterly antialiasing. Pixel character proportions like a classic 16-bit RPG farmer, roughly 16x32 or 24x40 sprite pixels: small normal-sized head, compact adult body, readable little boots and sleeves. The avatar has dark brown hair and a muted green shirt or hoodie, dark trousers. CAMERA: fixed slightly elevated RPG interior view, placed behind the character looking toward a desk against the north wall. Only the back of the character's head and back of clothing are visible in every state. No face, no eyes, no cheek/profile, no turning to camera. Overall board is a WIDE HORIZONTAL TRIPTYCH: three equally sized panels side by side, depicting the EXACT SAME cozy room with the EXACT SAME camera, furniture, palette and scale, and the same one employee in three different states. Three clean pixel-art Chinese labels above panels, exact text left '坐着打工', middle '摸鱼睡觉', right '站着打工'. No other interface panels, sidebars or controls. Each panel is an actual scene design. Furniture grounded in tiles and consistent perspective, inviting and calm. Warm sunny amber daytime lighting using stepped pixel color ramps.
